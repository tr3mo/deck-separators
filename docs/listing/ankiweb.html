Organize your deck list into clean, labeled sections. Deck Separators adds horizontal lines between your decks on the Decks screen, with an optional label such as "Languages" or "Semester 2". How to use Click a deck's gear icon, then Separator ▸ Above this deck or Below this deck. Use Label above… or Label below… to name it. Make it yours Separator ▸ Design… restyles every separator at once, with a live preview: • Line: solid, fade, dashed, dotted or double, 1–8 px, theme color or any custom color • Label: left, centered or right, as small caps, plain text or a pill • Spacing above and below each line Good to know • A separator belongs to its deck: it moves when you rename or move the deck, and syncs through AnkiWeb with your collection. AnkiMobile and AnkiDroid don't show separators. • Below this deck places the line after the deck and its open subdecks. • Works in light and dark mode. • Requires Anki 23.10 or newer. The legacy Qt5 builds of Anki are not supported. Screenshots and source: github.com/tr3mo/deck-separators
