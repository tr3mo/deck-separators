# Deck Separators: organize your deck list into labeled sections

Deck Separators adds horizontal lines between decks on the Decks screen, with an optional label.

![Deck list with Life Sciences, Languages and Hobbies sections](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tr3mo/deck-separators/main/docs/images/hero.png)

**How to use:** click a deck's gear icon, then **Separator ▸ Above this deck** or **Below this deck**. Labels are optional.

![The Separator submenu in the deck gear menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tr3mo/deck-separators/main/docs/images/gear-menu.png)

**Make it yours:** **Separator ▸ Design…** restyles every separator at once, with a live preview. Choose from five line styles (solid, fade, dashed, dotted, double), three label styles (small caps, plain, pill), left/centered/right labels, any color, and the spacing above and below.

![Six separator designs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tr3mo/deck-separators/main/docs/images/styles.png)

**Good to know**

- A separator belongs to its deck: it moves when you rename or move the deck, and syncs through AnkiWeb with your collection. AnkiMobile and AnkiDroid don't show separators.
- Works in light and dark mode.
- Requires Anki 23.10 or newer (the legacy Qt5 builds are not supported).
